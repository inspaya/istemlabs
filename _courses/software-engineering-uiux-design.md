## Objective
User Interface/User Experience design is crucial to software projects as this determines the first impression an end user has of your product. In this course, students will learn the laws guiding UI/UX design, the processes, tools, and how to conduct research for successful design. Through hands-on exercises, students go further to learn the differences between designing for different media like web and mobile.
